\chapter{Conclusion}\label{ch:conclusion}
% ============================================================================

This thesis set out to benchmark three families of implied-volatility smoothers and ended by
changing the question the benchmark asks. The parametric study replicated and validated the
Gatheral--Jacquier theory to the paper's own digits and quantified the fit--arbitrage window
($0.64$ vs $1.91,\vp$) that learning methods must enter. The deep-smoothing study entered it
and, on the way, produced the thesis's central object: a calendar penalty \emph{exactly}
zero at its collocation nodes coexisting with a $28\%$ violation rate between them. The
mechanism (exponential maturity spacing, correct for the butterfly constraint but structurally
blind for the calendar one, as formalised by the node-gap bound of
Theorem~\ref{prop:nodegap}), the repair (constraint-specific collocation; material
violations $34.4\%\to0.0\%$ on a gated stress), and the constructive counterpart (a prior
\emph{certified} arbitrage-free on its working range by the endpoint-monotonicity
Theorem~\ref{lem:range}) turn a caveat of the literature into a measured, repaired, and
certified methodology. The operator study showed the pathology is transversal and
two-dimensional, blind both between nodes and across days: the most accurate operator in the
study is materially butterfly-violating on $330$ of $386$ out-of-sample days while its own
training-grid audit reads clean. The downstream chapter priced it: through
$\sigma_{\mathrm{loc}}^2=\partial_\tau w/g$, the violation becomes a local-variance surface
requiring repair on half its domain, risk-neutral densities with up to $230\%$ negative mass,
and a measurable tax on residual-signal quality (the clean surface dominates its
arbitrage-dirty twin in every backtest cell). A detection-power experiment then shows that the
market's own spread structure prices out residual signals below $\approx3$ volatility
points, so the value of a clean surface lies in marking and mid-execution capture rather than
taker-side alpha. The final experiment supplied the missing consequence: on a path-dependent
down-and-out call, priced under an independent Heston ground truth, a node-aligned defect that
leaves the vanilla fit invariant to machine precision moves the exotic price by
$2$--$3\%$ of premium and its hedge by tens of percent, and the study's most accurate
operator---best vanilla RMSE---misprices the real barrier by $60\%$ off its half-invalid
local-volatility surface, while the prior-embedded operator recovers the clean price at no cost
in fit. The cost of a collocation blind spot is therefore not a free lunch the market failed to
remove; it is model risk realised on every exotic derived from the surface, and it is removed
constructively by a certified prior.

Three sentences summarise the contribution. \emph{Soft no-arbitrage constraints are
statements about sampling sets, and must be audited on sets they were not trained on, in
space and in time.} \emph{Where a closed-form certificate exists, use it constructively: a
certified prior is worth more than any penalty.} \emph{And always price the audit: the
objects a desk actually uses are ratios of exactly the derivatives the constraints control.}
The economic verdict is the same in one line: arbitrage violations in a fitted surface are not
a trading opportunity the market left on the table---it did not---but a model-risk liability
that surfaces the moment the surface is used to price and hedge anything path-dependent.

\paragraph{Future work.} The production run completes the empirical record (regime-stratified
five-year tables; the ``butterfly binds in stress'' hypothesis on Volmageddon/COVID weeks,
cross-referenced against the data-level scanner of Section~\ref{sec:scanner}; the full
residual-economics sample of Section~\ref{sec:residual}; VIX ground truth). Methodologically:
transporting constraints across days for operators (penalising on \emph{generated} query
days; certified projection layers as in hard-constraint approaches; or hybrid
operator-proposes/prior-projects architectures); adaptive collocation that provably bounds
the node-vs-audit gap, for which Theorem~\ref{prop:nodegap} is the natural starting
point; and the natural next object, operator smoothing \emph{of local volatility itself},
where Proposition~\ref{prop:dupire} makes the constraint set explicit. The recent SANOS line
\cite{sanos2026} suggests the non-parametric frontier is moving; the audit methodology of
this thesis applies to it verbatim.
